# Action Design System

## Overview

Action uses a print-editorial landing page organized as broad full-width chapters, alternating warm paper fields with dark technical panels. The visual identity combines field-manual illustration, precise construction geometry, sparse terminal UI, and generous negative space. Coral marks recording and focus; cyan marks live signal and machine state. Typography pairs an expressive book serif with compact monospaced operational labels.

## Colors

- **Paper**: `#f3ebdd` — primary warm background.
- **Paper Shadow**: `#dccfb9` — offset depth and secondary paper planes.
- **Paper Light**: `#faf5eb` — lifted panels and light text on dark fields.
- **Graphite Ink**: `#20282b` — primary text, rules, and dark sections.
- **Slate**: `#596261` — secondary copy and inactive machine state.
- **Recording Coral**: `#ef6a47` — record state, focus targets, and active cues.
- **Signal Cyan**: `#1fb9c6` — connectivity, verified state, and accessibility focus.
- **Field Tan**: `#a77850` — illustration leather and secondary warmth.
- **Workbench**: `#141a1c` — terminal and browser frames.
- **Video Black**: `#0c1113` — deepest media surface.

## Typography

- **Display Serif**: EB Garamond, 400–600. Hero and chapter headings; large, tightly tracked, sentence case. Hero scale is roughly 64–113px on desktop.
- **Operational Mono**: JetBrains Mono, 400–600. Labels, commands, lifecycle states, buttons, and artifact names. Usually 10–14px, uppercase, and letter-spaced.
- **Body**: EB Garamond at roughly 17–23px with relaxed leading.

## Elevation

Depth comes from flat offset paper or coral shadows, fine one-pixel rules, and changes between paper and graphite fields. Dark workbench panels use thin translucent borders and hard 14–18px offset shadows. Avoid glossy gradients and conventional blurred card shadows.

## Components

- **Field Illustration**: Paper-textured technical scenes with coral lens targets, graphite drafting lines, and sparse cyan signal points.
- **Workbench Panel**: Square-cornered dark command surface with a fine border and coral offset shadow.
- **Browser/Media Frame**: Inset dark viewport inside a thin ruled outer frame with small mono status labels.
- **Trace Lifecycle**: Four ruled paper cells for preflight, running, completing, and review.
- **Artifact Manifest**: Graphite code panel with cyan keys, warm values, and a paper offset shadow.
- **Operational Eyebrow**: Coral mono label, uppercase with a short horizontal rule.
- **Target Geometry**: Concentric circles, crosshairs, fine paths, and small status nodes.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Use large paper fields and deliberate negative space.
- Use coral only for recording, focus, and decisive emphasis.
- Keep UI geometry fine, square, and diagrammatic.
- Pair slow editorial camera motion with crisp operational state changes.
- Let mono labels clarify what the serif headline promises.

### Don'ts

- Do not use rounded SaaS cards or neon glassmorphism.
- Do not fill every region; the quiet paper is part of the identity.
- Do not use generic blue gradients or soft drop shadows.
- Do not animate every construction line at once.
- Do not imitate a raw desktop capture for the whole film.
