# Six other small movers

Six alternatives to Ledge, made with built-in image generation. Each starts from a different grid-born silhouette, with two clear eyes and a single running-green accent. These are exploratory masters.

Open the [comparison gallery](../index.html) to see every concept large and in 16, 24, and 32 px image frames on `#141416` and `#f2f2f2`. Each surface also includes an exact 24 px image inside a 32 px control. [prompts.json](prompts.json) records the exact prompts.

## Choice for a 24 px control

**Shim** is my alternative pick. Its uninterrupted wedge silhouette, broad pale face, and widely separated dark eyes have the least detail competing for the small image frame. The sloping green edge gives it an identity without needing hands or an object to explain it. Its low centre of gravity also fits a deliberate mover.

Ledge tells the carrying story more clearly. Nook keeps that story in a simpler body; Dov has the strongest inward waist; Ply is the closest connection to layers; Span makes supporting a load literal; Plinth feels the most settled.

This is a visual design recommendation from the saved masters, not a claim of native-app acceptance. The gallery exposes the small-size comparison for review. Final adoption should include a pixel-aligned 1×/2× export pass and an actual 32 pt native control check.

## Scope

Only the PNGs and static gallery are implemented. The [lead README](../README.md) contains the 2.5-second motion proposal and reduced-motion treatment. No character is wired into the app.
